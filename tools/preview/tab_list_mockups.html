Same in every option.

  • Everyone on the network appears in everyone's tab list, with their real skin and ping. Nothing shows the server (D16). The client keeps its vanilla alphabetical order and column layout.
  • Header, footer and the entry format are MiniMessage lines in messages.toml; placeholders <player>, <online>, <ping>, <prefix>, <staff>. The footer refreshes every few seconds.
  • /players (alias /online) is open to everyone and lists names only. With bspva.players.servers it is grouped by server with counts. Velocity's own /glist is untouched.
  • Velocitab comes out of plugins/ when this ships (D3); two plugins writing the same tab list would fight.
  • Players with bspva.joinleave.silent still appear in the tab list; hiding staff from it is a separate decision if ever wanted.
