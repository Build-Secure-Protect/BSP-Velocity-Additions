Season reset: the disconnect screen

When BSP-Core's /bsp season reset reaches the proxy, every player on the network is sent off with this screen. BSP-Core's own kick (from each backend) shows option A's words in gold, and whichever arrives first is the one a player sees. BSP-Core sends the starting season's number in the notice (request VA-C4); kickNoNumber is only for an older BSP-Core. Shown here with season 12.

A: one gold line

Bundled
Disconnected
Season 12 has begun! Everyone starts over. Log in again to get your fresh Shatter Totem.
Back to server list
[seasonReset]
kick = "<gold>Season <season> has begun! Everyone starts over. Log in again to get your fresh Shatter Totem."
kickNoNumber = "<gold>A new season has begun! Everyone starts over. Log in again to get your fresh Shatter Totem."

The same screen as BSP-Core's own kick, so every player sees one look whichever kick lands first.
